SNMP monitoring collects device health, performance, and alert data from routers, switches, servers, firewalls, and other infrastructure. By combining polling, traps, MIBs, OIDs, and secure SNMP versions, it helps IT administrators track network health, identify faults, and plan capacity across environments of any size.
